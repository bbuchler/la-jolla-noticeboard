Grades 7 and 11

Math and ELA Practice Tests: Due December 3

Every 7th and 11th grader takes two practice tests in Mastery Connect: one in Math and one in English Language Arts (ELA). You can take them from home. Plan to finish at least one before Thanksgiving break (November 23-27).

Why These Tests Matter

A preview of the state test In the spring, 7th and 11th graders take the state CAASPP test. These practice tests use the same kinds of questions and tools, so test day holds no surprises.
See where you stand They're called "predictive" because they estimate how ready you are for the state test right now, while there's still time to improve.
Prep that fits you Your teacher uses your results to plan your Math and ELA prep, so your time goes to the skills you actually need, not the ones you already know.
No pressure on your grade These tests don't affect your course grade. Nothing to study. Just work on your own and do your best.

How to Start a Test

You'll use your school Microsoft 365 login. Never logged in before? Set it up first with the Student Account Login Guide.

  1. Get readyUse a charged laptop or Chromebook in a quiet spot. Have a pencil and scratch paper. You don't have to finish in one sitting. You can pause and come back.
  2. Go to altusgo.com/student-portalThis is the same Student Portal you use for your other school apps.
  3. Click Clever and sign inClick Clever (Single Sign-On), then Log in with Microsoft Entra. Use your school email (ending in @stu.altusschools.net) and your password.
  4. Click Mastery ConnectYou'll find it with your other learning apps in Clever.
  5. Choose the test for YOUR gradeYou'll see tests for other grades too. Pick carefully using the list in Pick Your Test below, then click Start Assessment.
  6. Submit when you're doneCheck that you answered every question, click Finish, then Yes to submit. Then tell your teacher you finished.

7th Graders: ELA Listening Section

The 7th grade ELA test starts with a short listening section that your teacher gives live. Set up a time with your teacher (in person or Zoom) for that part. You can do the rest on your own.

Test Tips

Read every direction Some questions need more than one answer. Others ask you to type, drag, or click.
Calculator On math sections that allow one, the calculator is in the Tools menu on the right side of the screen. Some sections don't allow a calculator.
Need a break? Click the pause icon and close your browser. Log back in later and you'll start where you left off.
Screen frozen or internet dropped? Your answers are saved. Refresh the page and sign in again. Don't use the browser's back button during the test.
Don't submit early Once you click Finish and Yes, you can't go back in. If you click Finish by accident, choose No.
Your own work No outside help, websites, or AI tools. The results only help you if they show what you really know.

Get Help

Can't log in?

Try the steps in the Password Help section of the login guide. Still stuck? Contact your teacher right away so they can request a password reset before your test day.

Don't see Mastery Connect or your test?

Contact your teacher. They can check that your test is turned on and give you another way to start it.

Want to test with your teacher?

You're welcome to. Set up a time in person at the La Jolla Resource Center, 7456 Girard Ave., La Jolla, CA 92037, or on Zoom at bit.ly/buchlerzoom. Please schedule first. Your teacher isn't waiting in the Zoom room all day.
