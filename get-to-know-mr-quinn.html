How I Approach Teaching

I believe every student has the ability to succeed, regardless of the skill level they bring when they join Altus. With commitment, hard work, and a growth mindset, students can reach their full potential.

My goal is to help students become not only strong students, but also dependable, capable, and employable individuals prepared for their future.
