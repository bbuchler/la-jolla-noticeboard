Why Dual Enrollment?

Free college classes Tuition is waived for dual enrollment students. Other fees are usually handled through school support if they come up.
Real college credit When students pass, they earn college credit from the community college.
Weighted GPA Dual enrollment classes are graded on a 5-point scale, similar to an AP class.
Double high school credit Approved courses can satisfy more than one high school requirement. The spring flyer lists English as a full year of high school English and Personal Growth as Pathway Exhibition plus an elective/CTE credit.
Graduate with a head start Students who start early can finish high school with college classes already completed, sometimes close to a semester of college credit.
Confidence boost Students learn that they can handle a real college class before they graduate high school.

Read This Before You Sign Up

Sign up ASAP

These classes are popular and can fill quickly. If you know you want a course, start the application process now.

Plan for one college class

For most students, one dual enrollment course per semester is the right amount. A second course may be possible only with special approval.

You still have high school work

Dual enrollment means doing both: your college class and your regular Altus high school coursework. Be ready to manage both at the same time.

Best first course: Personal Growth

Career and Life Planning is usually the easiest place to begin because it helps with goals, planning, confidence, and high school credit.

Need Help Signing Up?

Get virtual help from Amanda Rondinelli, Early College Credit Program Assistant. Students can drop into Friday office hours or schedule a private appointment for application, registration, and college-success support.

New SDCCD Signup Steps: City, Mesa & Miramar

Already have an SDCCD student ID?

If you applied or enrolled at City, Mesa, or Miramar less than a year ago and have your 10-digit ID, the updated Altus guide says you only need a new Supplemental Application. Skip to that step. If it was longer ago or you are unsure, check with Amanda or admissions before starting another application.

  1. Start at SDCCD: choose your collegeUse the new CCCStart application through the SDCCD Start page. Sign in with your existing OpenCCC/CCCApply account if you have one; otherwise create an account.
  2. Complete identity verificationSDCCD now requires identity verification through ID.me or CA DMV Wallet. Follow the official setup tutorial. If you cannot complete verification, ask Amanda or college admissions for help.
  3. Choose the high school journey and Spring 2027Select that you are currently in K-12, your grade when the class begins, and the goal of taking college classes while in high school. Choose the correct term/program and complete your personal information and academic history, including your actual school and expected graduation date.
  4. Review carefully, then submitCheck your email address, school, grade, graduation date, and term before submitting. Corrections after submission must go through the college. Save your confirmation and CCCID; the CCCID is different from your 10-digit SDCCD student ID.
  5. Get your 10-digit SDCCD student IDAllow one business day for processing, according to the updated guide. Search your inbox and spam for SDCCD or Welcome to mySDCCD. If nothing arrives within a couple of days, tell your teacher. Follow the welcome email to activate mySDCCD.
  6. Submit a new Supplemental Application for Spring 2027Use your 10-digit student ID and the exact course title, code, and five-digit class number above. List Steven Sutherland as your high school counselor/contact, not your teacher: ssutherland@altusschools.net.
  7. Watch for enrollment confirmationCollege staff enroll high school students after the supplemental form is accepted. Check email and mySDCCD for your confirmed class. An application alone is not enrollment; tell your teacher if the class is full or you need help.

Coastline College

Coastline is best for students who want more variety. Availability changes, so check the current schedule and talk with your teacher before choosing.

Coastline Signup Steps

  1. Create or use your OpenCCC account. The OpenCCC account is the starting point for Coastline too.
  2. Apply to Coastline College. Coastline says processing usually takes 1-3 business days. Check your email daily for MyCoast login information.
  3. Sign in to MyCoast. Use the account information Coastline sends you.
  4. Complete the High School Release and Parent Consent steps. Do this with teacher guidance. You will need the course name, course number, and counselor information.

    Important: Do not use your teacher. Your counselor is Mr. Sutherland: ssutherland@altusschools.net.

  5. Register for your class in MyCoast. Register early so you do not get waitlisted.

Good starting categories

  • Career and life planning
  • College success
  • Stress management
  • Government
  • Art, music, and world language
  • Cybersecurity, Python, marketing, design, law, and teaching
