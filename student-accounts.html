Your School Microsoft 365 Account

One school login opens Clever, IXL, your learning apps, and school testing. Set it up once, pick a password you will remember, and you are ready.

Why This Account Matters

One login for everything Altus uses Clever for single sign-on. Sign in one time with your school account and your school apps and curriculum open without separate passwords.
IXL and your learning apps Your learning apps now use this school login. If it does not work, you cannot open your assignments.
Ready for testing You will use this account for upcoming school testing. Make sure your login works now, not on test day.
Free Microsoft apps Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Teams, and Outlook are included with your account.
Your work is saved in the cloud OneDrive stores your schoolwork online, so you can open your files from any school-approved device.
Reach your teacher Students in grades 6-12 get a school Outlook email account for messages with teachers. Teams works for meetings and group projects.

First Login: Step by Step

This takes about 5 minutes. Have your username and temporary password ready. Your teacher sends them to you.

  1. Find your username and temporary passwordYour username is your school email. It ends in @stu.altusschools.net. Your teacher gives you your temporary password.
  2. Go to altusgo.com and click Student PortalAltusGO is the starting page for all of your school accounts.
  3. Click Microsoft 365 and sign inEnter your school email and your temporary password.
  4. Create your new passwordMicrosoft asks you to change your password the first time you log in. Choose one you will remember and save it somewhere safe. Do not share it with anyone.
  5. Test CleverGo back to the Student Portal and click Clever (Single Sign-On). Choose Log in with Microsoft Entra and use your school email and new password.
  6. Open your learning appsIn Microsoft 365, click Apps to find IXL SSO. Other learning apps are listed in Clever under Learning Apps. If you cannot find an app your teacher assigned, let your teacher know.
  7. Tell your teacher it worksA quick reply to your teacher's email is enough.

Password Help

Forgot your password?

Contact your teacher. Your teacher sends a reset request to the school and gives you a new temporary password. Then log in and create a new password again.

Temporary password not working?

You may have already changed it. Try the password you created. If that does not work either, contact your teacher for a reset.

Wrong Microsoft account opens?

If Microsoft signs you in to a personal or family account, open a private browser window and sign in with your school email.

Save your password

Write it down somewhere safe at home or save it in a password manager. You will need it for every school app and for testing.

Keep Your Account Safe

Your Microsoft account is for school use only.

  • Do not share your username or password, even with friends.
  • Follow the Technology Use Agreement.
  • Be kind and respectful online.
  • Only use school tools for learning.
  • Your school helps keep accounts safe by monitoring activity.
